Oct 2026 Puur Reizen

Customer portal and back office on Odoo for Puur Reizen

For a travel agency: customers, trips, documents and invoices in one place, built on standard software.

  • Customers see their own trips, the itinerary from Wetu, what is still missing and what is still due, and upload their travel documents themselves.
  • The agency follows every trip on a kanban board per status, raises deposit, balance and extra invoices, and sends them through Peppol or Billit.
  • Nothing goes out without a check: reminders and status changes are proposals that a staff member confirms. An AI assistant gets read-only access and never sees identity documents.
  • Built and tested in a few days with AI agents: four modules for Odoo 20, a branding module and 115 automated tests.

Technology: Odoo 20, Python, PostgreSQL, Odoo.sh, Peppol, Billit API, Wetu API
